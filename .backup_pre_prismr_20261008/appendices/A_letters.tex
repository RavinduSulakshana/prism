\chapter{Permission Letters}\label{app:letters}

This appendix reproduces the letters through which permission for the aerial traffic survey at the Maviththara junction was requested and granted. Personal contact details of the drone operator (national identity card number, telephone number, e-mail address and home address) have been redacted. All other content, including official references and dates, is reproduced unaltered.
