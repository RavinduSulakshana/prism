\chapter{Additional Test Cases}\label{app:tests}

This appendix lists the supplementary test cases TC11--TC33, which complement the ten principal cases of \cref{tab:eval-tc} in \cref{ch:eval}. Together they cover every functional and non-functional requirement that is in scope (FR01--FR18, NFR01--NFR09). Columns are as in \cref{tab:eval-tc}; all measured values are from the final versions of the components. TC31--TC33 were added during the revision of the control law, and TC22 and TC23 were strengthened: as \cref{sec:eval-review} explains, their original forms verified that each component did what it had been told rather than that it had been told the right thing, and both passed while the defects they should have caught were present. Where a figure before the corresponding fix is known, it is given in parentheses.
